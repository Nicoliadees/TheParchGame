Starting my first indie game The Parch

The Parch is a top-down third person survival RPG set in a stylised diorama of the Pilbara outback. Stranded in the vast Ausralian outback, you, a lone survivor and your dog companion try to eke out an existance, If you can call it that. Scavenging what little resources you can off the bones of the old world all the while trying to stay dry. Using your battered old Land Crusier as a home and a means to traverse this vast outback landscape. Drive, explore, scavenge, repair and survive. Building an unbreakable mateship through shared dangers, dark Aussie humour and small hard-won victories in this beautiful but harsh landscape.
